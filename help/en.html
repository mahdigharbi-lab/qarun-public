Game introduction

Qarun’s Legacy is an incremental game set in space. You start in a small world where you buy items that produce coins over time, then you use what you earn to buy more expensive items and unlock new worlds, until you reach Qarun’s Treasure and beyond.

Each item produces a single batch per cycle. The batch waits for you until you harvest it, and no new batch builds up until you have harvested the old one. The more often you visit the game, the more you earn.

Currencies

  • Coins: produced by items; you use them to buy items and to upgrade your worlds.
  • Gems: a rare currency that comes from special buildings, daily rewards, events and the weekly ranking. They are used to buy premium items and keys, to upgrade worlds, to fuse items and to finish a wait instantly.
  • Dark matter: harvested only in World 9, and it appears in the bar once you own some.

You start with 100 coins and 100 gems. You can convert gems into coins from the gem shop; the conversion value rises as you progress through the worlds. Conversion is not available on the first day after registration.

The eight worlds

In order: First Orbit, Planet Trail, Golden Nebula, Tower of the Spheres, Galaxy of Essence, Throne of Stars, Horizon of Immortality, Qarun’s Treasure.

Each world has six groups of regular items and one premium item bought with gems, whose production equals the combined production of the six items. Each item has a maximum number of copies, set for you once when you first enter the world, and it may differ from one player to another. You own a world when you have bought all of its regular items.

Harvesting

When an item is ripe, a pulsing signal appears on it: click it to harvest. If you don’t want to wait, you can finish the ripening instantly for one gem per remaining hour. You can buy the global harvest once, which harvests all the worlds you own with a single click.

Fusion

If you have bought all the copies of an item, you can fuse them for gems, and fusion increases the item’s yield by 10%. The price of fusion varies by world and by item.

Keys

Seven keys open worlds 2 to 8, in order: Bronze, Iron, Copper, Silver, Gold, Red ruby, Diamond.

  • You may find the key by chance when you harvest, and the chance gets lower the higher the world.
  • If you don’t find it, you can buy it with gems once you have bought all the world’s items.
  • If you find the key in a world you own, the next world opens automatically.
  • Keys stay in your inventory until your next Prestige.

There is no key after world 8: World 9 opens with a puzzle, as explained below.

Upgrades and Prestige

World upgrade

Each world can be upgraded through levels, and each level increases the world’s production. You pay in coins or gems, and the price rises with the level. The upgrade window shows your current production and the production you will have after the next level.

Prestige

When you buy the regular items of world 7, the Prestige button appears. Prestige takes your progress back to the beginning in exchange for a permanent 50% increase in income, which builds up with each Prestige.

The Legendary Prestige appears in world 8, increases income by 200%, and can only be used once in the lifetime of the account.

When you Prestige, your coins go back to 100 and the keys must be collected again, and the gem conversion rate returns to its starting level. Your gems and dark matter stay with you. Special buildings stay bought but are locked until their requirements are met again, and the World 9 gate mark stays permanent.

Special buildings

Four buildings stand at the edges of the world map: two lighthouses and two star gates. You buy each building once with coins, after owning certain worlds, and it produces gems for you every day. The more advanced the stage a building belongs to, the higher its production. When it is ripe, click it to harvest.

Wonders and auctions

Seven wonders, each with a permanent effect on your game:

  • The Great Pyramid: lowers item prices by 10%.
  • The Hanging Gardens of Babylon: reduce ripening time by 10%.
  • The Temple of Artemis: raises the chance of finding keys by 15%.
  • The Colossus of Rhodes: raises the value of converting gems into coins by 20%.
  • The Statue of Zeus: increases the production of the first and third special buildings by 50%.
  • The Mausoleum of Mausolus: increases the production of the second and fourth special buildings by 50%.
  • The Lighthouse of Alexandria: increases the production of all four special buildings by 25%.

Auctions

Wonders are put up for auction, and an auction lasts 28 days. It starts at one million coins, and you bid any amount you choose. Nobody wins before the time is up; when it ends, the holder of the highest bid wins. The holder of the highest bid cannot outbid themselves, and the owner of the current wonder cannot take part in the auction for the whole month.

Lord of the Cosmos

Whoever gathers the seven wonders can fuse them: their effects are then doubled for 40 days, after which the wonders disappear, and the player holds the title of “Lord of the Cosmos”. The title stays with them, and their days of reign are counted, until another player takes it from them.

Events and statues

Eight events a year, each lasting a week: White Meteor, White Meteorite, Silver Meteor, Silver Meteorite, Brown Meteor, Brown Meteorite, Black Meteor, Black Meteorite. The event world appears in the middle of the map for its whole duration, and you can enter it once you have unlocked world 2.

Event items produce points instead of coins, the number of copies is the same for all players, and there is no fusion. You can finish ripening instantly with gems. Points give you rewards when you reach certain milestones, the top-ranked players receive extra rewards, and the first three get a statue. For the meteorite, rewards are doubled. When the event ends, your rewards are added to your balance automatically.

World 9

In world 8 there is a puzzle of eight pieces: you earn seven of them by buying the world’s items and completing the premium item, and you pay for the last piece with gems, its price being the sum of the prices of the seven keys. When the puzzle is complete, the World 9 gate opens.

World 9 is a grid of 60 × 60 squares covered by a fog that gradually recedes on dates announced in the news. You buy celestial bodies: the Moon, the planet, the star, the star system, the galaxy and the black hole. Each harvest gives you one unit of dark matter. Click a body that another player owns to see its owner and its yield.

Players are ranked by the dark matter they have harvested since the beginning, and the title “Richest of the Rich” is awarded to whoever tops the ranking; their days of wealth are counted.

Weekly ranking

The ranking is always active. Each week starts on Saturday at midnight, server time, and ends on Friday evening, then scores go back to zero. Your rank is calculated from the total coins you collected during the week from your regular and premium items and from legacy yield. In case of a tie, whoever reached the score first comes ahead. Rewards are only activated once the number of registered players reaches 1,000; the top 200 players then receive rewards according to their rank, added to their balance the first time they log in after the week ends.

Daily tasks and rewards

Three sections:

Login days

A seven-day cycle that repeats, and being away costs you nothing. From the first to the sixth day, you receive your worlds’ production for 15 minutes, then 30 minutes, then 1 hour, then 1 h 30, then 2 hours, then 3 hours. On the seventh day, you receive 3 gems.

Login streak

Missing a single day resets the count to zero. You receive one gem on the seventh day, two gems on the fourteenth day, and so on up to 10 gems on the seventieth day, then 10 gems at every multiple of seven.

Daily tasks

Nine tasks: buy an item, harvest 50 times, visit two worlds, harvest a certain amount of coins, earn or spend a gem, buy a premium item, fuse copies of an item, take part in an auction, and upgrade a world. Each task gives you five minutes of production; once five tasks are completed, you receive an extra reward: half an hour of production and a gem, and the same reward is granted a second time when all nine tasks are completed.

Don’t rush to claim the reward: the more your production grows, the more it is worth. Make sure to claim all rewards before midnight.

Legacy

If a player stays away from the game for a long time, their account is deleted, and their legacy is put up in the “legacy auction”, in which everyone takes part under the same rules. The winner gets the legacy icon: a scroll that bears the former owner’s nickname and earns an income at every midnight. Each legacy auction has its own icon, and you can win several auctions.

Rules

Account and nickname

Registration is done with an email, which you confirm with a code sent to you, or with a Google account. A player’s nickname is unique, with no difference between uppercase and lowercase letters. A guest plays in world 1 only and cannot unlock world 2 before registering. Changing your nickname is free 30 days after registration; before that, it costs 50 gems.

Content

Your nickname, picture or description must not be offensive. The profile description cannot exceed 150 characters and cannot contain links.

Fair play

Exploiting game bugs is forbidden, and so is using any automated tool. The team may take appropriate action against accounts that break these rules.

Inactive accounts

The account of a player who has never bought from the gem shop is deleted after 200 days of inactivity; the account of a player who has bought from it, even once, is deleted after 300 days. Their legacy is then put up for auction, as described in the “Legacy” section. Fifteen days before deletion, a warning email is sent to you; logging in again is enough to keep your account.
