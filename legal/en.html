Terms of use

Last updated: November 19, 2026

1. The publisher and the purpose of the game

Qarun’s Legacy (“the game”) is a free browser game, published in a personal capacity by its creator, who is based in Tunisia. Contact: qarunheritage@gmail.com. By playing, you accept these terms and the game rules, which appear at the end of the player’s guide.

2. Minimum age

The game is for people aged 13 and over. If you are under 18, you must have the permission of one of your parents or your legal guardian.

3. Accounts

You can play as a guest (in the first world only) or create an account with an email address confirmed by a code, or with a Google account. You are responsible for keeping your password confidential and for what is done with your account. Your nickname is unique, with no difference between uppercase and lowercase letters. Changing your nickname is free 30 days after registration, and costs 50 gems before that.

4. Content and behavior

Your nickname, profile picture and description must not be offensive. The description cannot exceed 150 characters and cannot contain links. Exploiting game bugs and using automated tools are forbidden. The publisher may take any appropriate action against an account that breaks these rules, including deleting it.

5. Virtual items

Coins, gems, dark matter, buildings, legacies and the other items in the game are virtual. They have no monetary value, cannot be exchanged for money, and remain the property of the publisher, who grants you the right to use them within the game only. The game currently offers no purchases with real money. If that ever changes, specific purchase terms will be published beforehand.

6. Inactive accounts and legacy

  • Guest accounts and registered accounts whose email address has not been confirmed are deleted after 15 days.
  • A registered, confirmed account is deleted after 200 days without logging in, or 300 days if the player has ever bought gems with real money.
  • Fifteen days before deletion for inactivity, a warning email is sent to the account’s address. Logging in again is enough to keep your account, so it is important to keep a valid address.
  • When an account is deleted for inactivity, its nickname and its daily income are put up in a Legacy auction, open to all players.

7. Deletion on request

The game has no account deletion button. Write to qarunheritage@gmail.com from your account’s address to request deletion.

8. Availability and liability

The game is provided “as is”, with no guarantee of continuous availability. It may be changed, interrupted or shut down, and progress may be adjusted for game balance or lost in the event of a failure. To the extent permitted by law, the publisher is not liable for indirect damages related to the use of the game.

9. Changes

These terms may change. The update date appears at the top of this section. Continuing to play after a change means you accept it.

10. Governing law

These terms are governed by Tunisian law, without prejudice to the rights that the law of your country of residence mandatorily grants you.

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

1. Data controller

The publisher of the game (see the terms of use), reachable at qarunheritage@gmail.com.

2. Data collected

  • Guest: an internal nickname generated by the game, and your progress.
  • Registered account: email address, password (stored in hashed form, never in plain text), nickname, chosen profile picture, description (optional), and the dates of your registration, last login and last nickname change.
  • Google account: the Google identifier, email address and name linked to the account, verified with Google; no password is received.
  • Portal account (CrazyGames, Y8): the identifier given by the portal.
  • Game data: progress, balances, achievements, rankings, legacies and tasks.
  • The game does not record your IP address in its own database. The hosting providers may, however, keep technical logs (see point 5).

3. Why we use this data

To run the game and your account, send you confirmation codes, address-change codes and password-reset codes, warn you before a deletion for inactivity, display the rankings, ensure fair play and answer your requests. This data is neither sold nor used for advertising.

4. What other players see

Your nickname, profile picture, description, account age and game results (rankings, achievements, titles) are visible to other players. Your email address never is. If your account is deleted for inactivity, your nickname appears in the Legacy auction announcement.

5. Service providers and where data is stored

  • Render hosts the game server (Frankfurt region, Germany).
  • Supabase hosts the database (Stockholm region, Sweden).
  • Brevo sends our service emails (a European company).
  • Google handles sign-in with a Google account and provides the fonts (Google Fonts): when loading these fonts, your browser shares your IP address with Google.
  • When the game is offered on a game portal (for example CrazyGames, Y8 or Poki), that portal applies its own privacy policy.

Portal accounts. When you play on a game portal such as CrazyGames or Y8, you can sign in with your portal account. We then receive an identifier from the portal and, where provided, your portal nickname. We never receive your portal password. On Y8, the game loads Y8’s sign-in script, so Y8 may receive your IP address.

Our main providers are located in the European Union.

6. How long we keep data

Your data is kept as long as your account exists, then deleted with it: after 15 days for guests and unconfirmed accounts, after 200 or 300 days of inactivity (see the terms), or at your request. Confirmation and reset codes expire quickly.

7. Storage in your browser

The game uses your browser’s local storage to remember your language, your sound and animation settings, the last announcement read and your session. It is not used to track you and contains no advertising trackers.

8. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data by writing to qarunheritage@gmail.com from your account’s address. We reply within 30 days.

9. Security and minors

Passwords are hashed and communications use HTTPS. No system is infallible. The game is for people aged 13 and over, and we delete the account of anyone we learn is using it below that age.
